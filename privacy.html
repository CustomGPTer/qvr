Privacy policy

QVR has no internet access and no servers. Your recordings stay on your phone.

In short

The developer collects nothing. QVR has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This website has none either.

What stays on your phone

Your recordings, recording icons and settings are kept on the phone. Private recordings are kept inside QVR and are deleted if you uninstall it or clear its data, so export anything you want to keep.

Location

If you switch on Store location data, QVR adds a recent position to new video files, on the phone. It is off unless you turn it on. Remember that location travels with a video you share.

Children

QVR is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 21 November 2026.
