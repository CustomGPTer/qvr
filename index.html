How it works

Set it up once, then record from your home screen.

  1. Make a recording icon

    In Settings, choose the back or front camera, a name and a picture, then add the icon to your home screen.

  2. Tap to record

    Recording starts straight away, with the quality and sound you chose. The screen can stay off.

  3. Tap again to save

    The video is saved to your folder, or privately inside QVR, ready to play, share or export.

Use it within the law

QVR apps record video and sound, and can keep recording with the screen off. Who and where you record, and what you do with the recordings, is your responsibility.

  • UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Recording identifiable people is processing their personal data. If you record beyond your own home and private life, data protection law is likely to apply to you. The Information Commissioner's Office has guidance at ico.org.uk.
  • Protection from Harassment Act 1997. Using a camera to watch, follow or keep a record of someone can be harassment.
  • Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 67 (voyeurism). Recording people in places where they expect privacy, such as a bathroom, a changing room or a bedroom, is a crime.
  • Covert recording. Recording someone without their knowledge can break data protection law, workplace rules and the rules of the place you are in, and recordings made that way may not be accepted as evidence. If in doubt, tell people they are being recorded.

Laws differ in other countries. If you are not in the UK, the rules where you are apply. Read the terms of use.

Installing and updating

QVR is not on Google Play, so your phone asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Download the app file

    Tap Download on your phone. The file is called QVR.apk and is about 0.5 MB. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, choose Download anyway. First tick the box to say you will use the app within the law.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow your browser to install apps. Allow it, go back and tap Install. If Google Play Protect steps in, choose More details, then Install anyway.

  3. Updating later

    QVR tells you when a newer version is out. Install the new file over the old one and everything you have saved is kept.

Questions

Is QVR free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription and no adverts.

Is QVR on Google Play?

No. It is downloaded from this site as an Android app file. It needs Android 10 or later.

Does it really record with the screen off?

Yes. Android shows a notification and its camera and microphone dots while QVR records, and you cannot turn those off.

Where are my videos?

In the folder you chose, or privately inside QVR if Hide video from system is on. Private recordings are deleted if you uninstall QVR, so export them first.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. QVR never uses the internet.

How do I update QVR?

Download the new version from this site and install it over the one you have. Do not uninstall first, or private recordings are lost.
